# Wavecrest Pro — User Acquisition Strategy

## TL;DR — Best 3 moves right now
1. Post in creator subreddits with a *useful* post (not an ad)
2. Launch on ProductHunt with a compelling hook
3. Daily "top trend" tweet that links to wavecrest.pro

---

## 1. Reddit (free, high-intent traffic)

Target these communities with genuinely helpful posts — **never just drop a link**:

- **r/NewTubers** (250k) — "I scraped YouTube trending daily for 30 days, here's what I found"
- **r/TikTokCreators** — post a breakdown of a trend that blew up, show the data
- **r/InstagramMarketing** — "Cross-platform trend analysis: what hits YouTube first tends to..."
- **r/juststart** — "Building a trend prediction SaaS in public — here's week 1"
- **r/SideProject** — post your build story
- **r/EntrepreneurRideAlong** — authentic "built this in X weeks" story

**The playbook:** Write a *value-first* post ("Here are the 10 trends dominating YouTube right now and why") — mention Wavecrest Pro at the bottom as the tool you used. Do this weekly.

---

## 2. ProductHunt Launch

ProductHunt can deliver 500-2,000 visitors in a single day if you do it right.

**Prep checklist:**
- [ ] Get 5-10 friends to upvote within the first hour (timing is everything)
- [ ] Write a killer tagline: "Know what's trending on YouTube/TikTok/IG 24hrs before everyone else"
- [ ] Make a 60-second demo GIF showing dashboard → trend spotted → content idea
- [ ] Post Tuesday–Thursday, 12:01am PT for max exposure
- [ ] Respond to every comment within an hour of posting

**Tagline ideas:**
- "The trend radar for serious creators"
- "Catch viral trends before they peak"
- "Your daily edge as a content creator"

---

## 3. Twitter/X Content Marketing

Post daily trend snapshots — free content marketing that builds brand + drives signups:

**Template tweet:**
> 🌊 Wavecrest Daily — Top YouTube trends right now:
>
> 🔥 [Trend 1]  
> 📈 [Trend 2]  
> ⚡ [Trend 3]
>
> Full breakdown at wavecrest.pro

This doubles as social proof. Anyone who sees it thinks "this tool actually tracks stuff in real time."

Add a cron job or GitHub Action that auto-posts the top 3 daily trends via Twitter API (free tier).

---

## 4. Creator-Focused Discord Servers

Join, contribute, *then* mention Wavecrest when relevant:

- Creator Clash Discord
- Various niche creator Discord servers (gaming, beauty, finance)
- Find them via Discord.gg directories

Strategy: Answer questions about trending topics using your own data, then say "I actually built a tool for this."

---

## 5. Referral Program (once you have ~50 users)

Give users a reason to share:

- "Refer 3 creators, get 1 month Pro free"
- Generate a `wavecrest.pro/ref/[username]` link on the dashboard
- Track signups via a `ref=` query param at signup

This is the highest-leverage long-term play. Creators already share tools in communities with each other.

---

## 6. SEO Blog Posts

Low effort, compounds over time. Target:

- "best YouTube trend tools 2026"
- "how to find trending topics on TikTok"
- "youtube trending topics today"
- "tiktok trend prediction tool"

Write 2-3 solid articles and submit to Google Search Console. Takes 3-4 months to kick in but it's free forever.

---

## 7. YouTube Creator Newsletters

Pitch these newsletters for a feature or sponsored spot (many are cheap or free if your product is relevant):

- **Creator Economy Newsletter** (Simon Owens)
- **Newsletter Crew**
- **Inbox Collective**
- **Matt Navara's social media newsletter**

Email: "I built a trend prediction tool for creators, would love to get your feedback / offer your readers a free trial."

## 8. "Beta Tester" Viral Loop (you already have this!)

Your beta page is underutilized. Double down:

- Add social sharing: "I'm an early beta tester at Wavecrest Pro — get free Pro access while spots last → [link]"
- Add a counter: "237 beta spots remaining" (urgency works)
- Tweet your beta link with a clear hook
