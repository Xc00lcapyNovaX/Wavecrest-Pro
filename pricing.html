Common questions

FAQ

Do credits expire?

Never. Credits stay in your account until you use them. Buy a pack today, use it six months from now — it works exactly the same.

What counts as one analysis?

One creator channel, fully decoded: up to 200 videos scanned, 7 dimensions surfaced, a full playbook generated with PDF export and shareable link. Each credit = one of those.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube is live now — paste a channel URL, a video URL, or an @handle. TikTok and Instagram are next and Agency plan users get early access when they launch.

How long does an analysis take?

Under 90 seconds for most channels. Channels with 1,000+ videos may take a couple of minutes — we cap analysis at the 200 most recent videos so the focus stays on what's working now.

Can I share reports with clients or my team?

Every report gets a shareable link anyone can view without an account. Agency plan users also get white-label PDFs with your branding instead of ours.

Is there a trial for the Agency plan?

Email hello@wavecrest.pro — we'll set up a 2-week trial for your team, no card needed.
