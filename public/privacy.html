Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026  ·  Effective: April 18, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Wavecrest Pro ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our service at wavecrest.pro.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by your use of the service, and information from third parties:

Category What we collect Why
Account info Email address, name, password (hashed), profile photo (OAuth) To create and manage your account
Onboarding Platform focus, content niche, creator goal To personalize your trend feed
Usage data API calls made, features accessed, search queries, timestamps To enforce rate limits and improve the service
Billing Payment method, subscription plan (via Stripe — we never store raw card details) To process payments
OAuth providers Google or GitHub profile ID and email (if you sign in via OAuth) To authenticate you without a password
Technical data IP address, browser type, session tokens Security and session management

We do not collect your actual content, videos, or social media posts. We only track which trend topics you search for within Wavecrest Pro.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing the service: Showing you personalized trend data, enforcing your plan limits, processing searches
  • Account management: Sending verification emails, magic links, and billing receipts
  • Improving Wavecrest Pro: Analyzing which features are used, fixing bugs, improving trend accuracy
  • Communications: Sending important service updates and, if you opt in, product newsletters
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data to train AI models sold to others.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We share your data with service providers only as necessary to operate the platform:

  • Stripe — Payments. Stripe processes your payment information and is PCI-DSS compliant. We store only your Stripe customer ID. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Resend — Transactional email (verification links, magic links). Resend Privacy Policy
  • Neon — Database hosting (your account data is stored here). Neon operates on AWS infrastructure. Neon Privacy Policy
  • Google / GitHub OAuth — Only if you choose to sign in via OAuth. We receive your email and profile name only.
  • Vercel — Web hosting and serverless infrastructure.

All third-party providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

4. Cookies and Sessions

We use a single session cookie (connect.sid) to keep you logged in. This cookie is:

  • HttpOnly (inaccessible to JavaScript)
  • Secure (only sent over HTTPS in production)
  • Session-scoped — it expires when you log out

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, or analytics cookies (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel).

5. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Usage logs: Retained for up to 90 days for rate limiting and security purposes, then deleted.
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations (held by Stripe).
  • Email tokens: Expire within 15 minutes (magic links) or 24 hours (verification links) and are purged after use.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update incorrect information in your account settings
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt out: Unsubscribe from non-essential emails at any time

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@wavecrest.pro. We will respond within 30 days.

7. GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Contract performance: Processing necessary to deliver the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests: Security, fraud prevention, and service improvement
  • Consent: Optional communications (you can withdraw consent at any time)

8. Children's Privacy

Wavecrest Pro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including:

  • Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes (never in plain text)
  • All connections encrypted over HTTPS/TLS
  • Database access restricted to the application server
  • Session tokens are rotated and HttpOnly

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify you promptly if a data breach affects your account.

10. International Data Transfers

Wavecrest Pro is hosted on infrastructure located primarily in the United States. If you are accessing the service from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the US, which may have different data protection laws than your country.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or a prominent notice on our website at least 14 days before the change takes effect. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns:

  • Email: privacy@wavecrest.pro
  • General support: support@wavecrest.pro
