v0.4.0

Editorial redesign + Fraunces

June 17, 2026

Design Improved

New font: Swapped Playfair Display for Fraunces across the site — variable optical sizing means it looks sharp at display sizes and clean at body sizes simultaneously.

Performance: Trimmed dead CSS, removed orphaned cursor JS (~200 lines), tightened animation sequencing.

Mobile nav: Hamburger-to-X morphing animation, aria-expanded wired correctly, Sign in + Start free hidden on mobile to avoid overflow.

Dossier dates: Masthead and dossier timestamps are now both driven from the same new Date() call — no more drift between them.

v0.3.2

Copy pass + product clarity

June 10, 2026

Improved Fixed

Positioning shift: Moved from "lead delivery" to "outbound OS with prospect memory." The core differentiator — memory that compounds — is now front and center throughout.

Hero copy: New headline "Never lose a lead again. Not one." Removed the introductory pill, which felt like friction before the headline.

Step 4 added to How it works: "Wavecrest remembers everyone — re-engage when the moment is right."

Fixed: Ghost problem numbers (6.2, 73%) were invisible on light background — color corrected.

Fixed: Floating stat badges clipping off-screen on mid-width viewports — replaced with inline stats row.

v0.3.0

Team features + shared memory

June 1, 2026

New

Shared prospect memory: On Team plan, all reps share one memory layer — no two AEs reach out to the same contact.

Manager view: Pipeline overview showing each rep's daily brief status, emails sent, and reply rate.

Territory splits: Segment prospects by geography, vertical, or company size — each AE gets a brief scoped to their territory.

Slack alerts: Re-engage signals fire to a Slack channel of your choice — so the whole team sees when a shared contact goes warm.

v0.2.5

Re-engage alerts + credit packs

May 20, 2026

New Improved

Re-engage alerts: Wavecrest now watches every contact you've ever reached out to. When they get promoted, raise a round, or revisit your pricing page — you get an alert with a one-click re-engage draft.

Credit packs launched: Starter ($9/25), Growth ($29/100), Pro ($89/400). One-time, no expiry. No subscription for individuals.

Pricing page visit tracking: Anonymous visitors to your company's pricing page can now be matched to known contacts in your memory layer and trigger a re-engage alert.

Signal labels: Each prospect in the brief now shows a clear signal tag — Funding, Hiring SDRs, New exec, Intent, Warm — so you know why they're in today's brief at a glance.

v0.2.0

Send from brief + CRM sync

May 5, 2026

New

Send from brief: One click sends from your Gmail or Outlook directly inside Wavecrest. No switching tabs, no copy-pasting. Logged to CRM automatically.

HubSpot + Salesforce sync: Every email sent from a brief creates a logged activity. Replies sync back. Your CRM stays clean without manual entry.

Follow-up timer: After sending, Wavecrest starts a follow-up countdown. Miss the window and it nudges you in tomorrow's brief.

Voice training: Paste 3–5 emails you've written. Wavecrest learns your tone — punchy, formal, direct — and writes every draft to sound like you.

v0.1.0

Initial launch

April 21, 2026

New

Daily brief: Every morning at 6 AM, a curated brief of prospects matched to your ICP with real buying signals. One per day on the free tier.

AI drafts: Each prospect comes with a ready-to-send email. Grounded in the signal, written in plain language, structured to get a reply.

ICP setup: 4-minute onboarding — industry, company size, titles, geography. Updates take effect in the next morning's brief.

Signal sources: Job postings, funding announcements, LinkedIn activity, tech stack changes, intent signals. 47,000+ data points processed nightly per user.

Free tier: 1 prospect per day, free forever. No card required.
